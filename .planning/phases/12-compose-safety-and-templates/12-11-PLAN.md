**Implements:** [docktor-app/docktor#19](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/19) — decision D-08 (passive "template updated" badge) and periodic template refresh.

<objective>
Keep template repos fresh in the background and tell users — passively — when the template their stack came from has changed. Tracer (Task 1): the sync job re-syncs a stale repo whose pinned variant changed, flips `templateUpdateAvailable` for that stack, and the stack header shows the blue "template updated" badge. Task 2 hardens the job (per-repo isolation, removed variants, unchanged stacks, registry order) and pins the no-auto-apply guarantee with tests.

Output: TemplateUpdateService, TemplateRepoSync job, badge.
</objective>
