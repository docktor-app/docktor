**Implements:** [docktor-app/docktor#23](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/23) — makes 14-06's probes correct in the real deployment: the probe overrides Docker's healthcheck (D-07) instead of being overwritten by StatePoller every 60 seconds, and Docktor can actually reach a stack's containers across Docker's per-network isolation (amended D-05, RESEARCH Finding 1).

No SPEC.md exists for this phase, so no probe-derived predicates were generated; requirements come from the GitHub issue with decisions locked in CONTEXT.md (Post-Research Amendments, 2026-10-07).

<objective>
Close the two gaps between "the probe engine works" (14-06, with 14-13's lifecycle) and "the probe works on a real install".

Purpose: (1) D-07 says the probe becomes the service's sole health signal. Since 14-01 and 14-11, StatePoller and the post-deploy catch-up write Docker's real health on every event and every 60-second reconcile, so without an ownership rule a probed service would flip between probe health and Docker health (or null) every minute, flapping incidents and cancelling notification timers. (2) Docker only routes between different bridge networks through published ports, and Docktor runs on its own compose network; the amended D-05 has Docktor join the target network for the duration of each probe and leave again.

Output: an ownership port + in-memory registry wired into StatePoller, the catch-up and the probe job; two new Docker port methods; refcounted ephemeral attachment, network preference and a crash-safe startup sweep in the probe transport.
</objective>
