**Implements:** [docktor-app/docktor#24](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/24) — the server half of the per-stack uptime view: the uptime percentage over a configurable retention window (D-09, D-10), the incident list backed by the existing `StackIncident` model (D-11), and retention pruning of the Phase 14 history tables.

No SPEC.md exists for this phase, so no probe-derived predicates were generated; requirements come from the GitHub issue with decisions locked in CONTEXT.md.

<objective>
Answer, for every stack, "what share of the observed time in the retention window was it actually up, and when exactly was it down?" — through two authenticated read endpoints, a retention setting, an incident writer and a daily prune job.

Purpose: #24 needs uptime and incidents over a window. D-09 fixes what counts as down (UNHEALTHY/ERROR only, maintenance excluded), D-10 fixes the window (global, 30-day default, 1-365), D-11 fixes the incident store (the scaffolded `StackIncident` table, one row per episode). RESEARCH Finding 6 supplies the correctness details: uptime comes from `StatusLog` intervals (never incident durations), the `StackIncident` unique key cannot stop duplicate open rows (NULLs are distinct), and status events for one stack arrive concurrently, so writes are serialized per stack.

Planner decision (CONTEXT discretion on prune mechanics, RESEARCH A9): `StatusLog` is NOT pruned. D-11 keeps it as-is because it also feeds the activity timeline; pruning it would irreversibly shorten that existing history on upgrade. Instead it gets a `(stackId, createdAt)` index so uptime reads stay indexed as it grows. The pruner deletes only the two Phase 14 history tables.

Output: one index migration, two pure domain modules, two repositories (+1 method on 14-01's repository), one read service, one incident tracker + subscriber, settings additions, two routes on 14-01's health route file, one daily job. 14-07 renders the Overview card and incident list from these endpoints; 14-10 renders the stack-list column and the retention card.
</objective>
