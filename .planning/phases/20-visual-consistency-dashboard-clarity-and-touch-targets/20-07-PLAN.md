**GitHub issues:** [#85 An honest backup card](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/85) (DASH-01, DASH-02) and the KPI part of [#90](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/90) (UI-03), bundle [#96](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/96).

<objective>
Replace the misleading "Backups Configured" card with a "Backup Coverage" card that counts stacks with a schedule or at least one snapshot, fed by a new server-derived flag on the stack list, and render dashboard KPIs in the mono font.

Per CONTEXT D-03 (one card, `n/total`, schedule or snapshot), D-04 (`hasBackup` on the server stack-list response from Backup records inside the backup repository, exposed through the stack service, no extra request, pure `computeDashboardStats`, em-dash error behaviour kept) and D-06 (KPI numbers and stat-card labels in mono). RESEARCH Pattern 2 gives the layering (read port on `StackService`, 11th constructor argument, enforced by `server/test/unit/architecture/layering.test.ts`) and Pitfalls 4-6. There is no stack-list schema in `@docktor/shared`, so the type lands on the client `Stack` interface (RESEARCH Pattern 2). This plan runs in wave 2 because plan 20-01 also edits `stacks.spec.ts`.

Known limitation to record in the SUMMARY (D-04 chose Backup records over restic): a stack whose restic repository was populated outside Docktor, or whose snapshots predate the Backup table, reads hasBackup false.

Purpose: the dashboard says exactly what it counts and no backed-up stack is invisible.
Output: repository method, service port and merge, wiring, client type, stats, StatCard hint and mono, card, hook freshness, unit tests, a CI integration test and the Playwright dashboard update.
</objective>
