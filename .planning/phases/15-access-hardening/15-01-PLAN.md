**Implements:** [docktor-app/docktor#46](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/46) (rate limiting on auth endpoints) and the server baseline of [docktor-app/docktor#45](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/45) (TOTP 2FA with recovery codes).

<objective>
Turn better-auth's own protections on for real and add the TOTP plugin, so the auth boundary is rate limited by a trustworthy client IP and 2FA sign-in works against the real server.

Purpose: RESEARCH found that the limiter is silently off today (disabled outside production, and skipped whenever no IP header is present), that X-Forwarded-For is spoofable, and that the origin check switches itself off under NODE_ENV=test. This plan closes those traps (D-11, D-12, D-13) and adds the twoFactor plugin with its native second step, trust-device cookie, recovery codes and password re-entry (D-04, D-05, D-06, D-07). Everything later in the phase builds on it: the mandatory-2FA gate and session revocation (15-02), the login second step (15-03), CSRF and cookie flags (15-04), and the Account page (15-05, 15-06).

Output: two Prisma models plus User.twoFactorEnabled and one generated migration, a pure auth-config module, the client-IP shim, the twoFactor plugin configuration, shared code/password schemas for the client forms, a TOTP test helper, and integration suites for rate limiting and the 2FA flow (run in CI).
</objective>
