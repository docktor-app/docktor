<objective>
Show, on every stack's Overview tab, how available the stack has been over the retention window, how many times it went down and for how long, and how much disk its volumes use — where users already look at a stack, without a separate Health page (D-16).

Purpose: 14-04 computes uptime from StatusLog intervals and serves the incident list; 14-12 records one StackIncident per episode; 14-02 measures volumes daily. UI-SPEC section C fixes the row of three StatCards, the truncating percentage format, the tone thresholds and the incident table. This plan wires them into the Overview with one request per stack, refreshed whenever the stack's live status changes.

Output: an API client, two pure formatters, two hooks, two section components, the OverviewTab composition, a client type extension, a default Playwright stub and one e2e spec.
</objective>
