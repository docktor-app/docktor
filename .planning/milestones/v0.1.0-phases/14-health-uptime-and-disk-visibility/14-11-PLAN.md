**Implements:** [docktor-app/docktor#23](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/23) — the health-state stability fix (RESEARCH Finding 4) that #23's notification criterion and #24's uptime depend on, plus recording of every Docker-health transition StatePoller and the catch-up observe.

No SPEC.md exists for this phase, so no probe-derived predicates were generated; requirements come from the GitHub issue with decisions locked in CONTEXT.md.

<objective>
Stop the 60-second reconcile from wiping every service's health, so HEALTHY/UNHEALTHY stacks stay put and the health history records every transition Docktor actually sees.

Purpose: `StatePoller.reconcile()` writes a hardcoded null health for every service every 60s (RESEARCH Finding 4). That flaps HEALTHY/UNHEALTHY stacks to RUNNING and cancels NotificationWatcher's UNHEALTHY timer, which breaks #23 criterion 4 and makes #24's uptime meaningless. 14-01 built the `service.health_changed` → `ServiceHealthEvent` pipeline from the handleEvent inspect path. This plan feeds it from reconcile, from handleEvent's 404 path and from the post-deploy catch-up.

Re-slice note (2026-10-07 revision): this was 14-01's Task 2. It was moved verbatim into its own expansion plan because 14-01's combined estimate (115k) exceeded the 100k smart-zone budget. 14-01 is the tracer slice; this plan expands it.

Output: reconcile and catch-up changes in two existing files plus their unit tests. No new modules, events or schema.
</objective>
