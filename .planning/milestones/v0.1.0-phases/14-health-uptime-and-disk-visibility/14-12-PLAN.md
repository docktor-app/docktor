**Implements:** [docktor-app/docktor#24](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/24) — completes the server half of the per-stack uptime view: the incident writer behind the incident list, backed by the existing `StackIncident` model (D-11), and retention pruning of the Phase 14 history tables (D-10).

No SPEC.md exists for this phase, so no probe-derived predicates were generated; requirements come from the GitHub issue with decisions locked in CONTEXT.md.

<objective>
Record every UNHEALTHY/ERROR episode of a stack as exactly one incident with a start and an end. Keep the Phase 14 history inside the configured retention window.

Purpose: 14-04 serves uptime and the incident list, but nothing writes `StackIncident` rows yet, and nothing deletes history older than the window.
- D-11 fixes the incident store: the scaffolded `StackIncident` table, one row per episode.
- D-10 says older rows are pruned by a scheduled job.
- RESEARCH Finding 6: the `StackIncident` unique key cannot stop duplicate open rows (NULLs are distinct), and status events for one stack arrive concurrently. Writes are therefore serialized per stack.

Re-slice note (2026-10-07 revision): this plan is 14-04's former Tasks 2 and 3, moved verbatim. 14-04's combined estimate (125k, 31 files) exceeded the 100k smart-zone budget. 14-04 is the tracer slice of #24's server side; this plan is its expansion.

Planner decision (CONTEXT discretion on prune mechanics, RESEARCH A9; recorded in 14-04): `StatusLog` is NOT pruned. D-11 keeps it as-is because it also feeds the activity timeline. 14-04 indexes it instead, and the pruner deletes only the two Phase 14 history tables.

Output: one pure domain module, write/delete methods on two repositories, one incident tracker + subscriber with its registration, and one daily job. 14-06's probe-driven status changes reach the tracker through the existing `stack.container_state_changed` event.
</objective>
