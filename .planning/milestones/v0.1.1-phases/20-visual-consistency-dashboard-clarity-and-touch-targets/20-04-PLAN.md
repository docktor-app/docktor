**GitHub issues:** [#92 One form spacing rule](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/92) (UI-05, bundle [#96](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/96)).

<objective>
Introduce the shared form layout primitives and move the auth forms, the create-stack form, the health-probe form and the setup wizard onto them, so label-to-input and field-to-button spacing follow one rule.

Per CONTEXT D-09 (shared primitives in `components/common/` own the spacing; `components/ui/form.tsx` untouched; no react-hook-form migration of Settings cards) and UI-SPEC R-04 (8px label to input, 16px field to field, 24px last field to actions). Planner decision for RESEARCH Open Question 2: keep 8px. The dialogs (enroll, disable two-factor, regenerate codes, backup schedule, proxy assign) and the certificates upload form adopt the same primitives in plan 20-09; Settings cards other than certificates are not touched by this phase ("touched in passing" only, D-09). The import flows contain no `<form>` element (grep-verified), so "create/import" is covered by the create-stack form.

Purpose: forms across the app share one rhythm, defined in one place.
Output: `form-layout.tsx` with tests, eleven adopting forms, and layout assertions.
</objective>

<interfaces>
Target contract (this plan):
- `interface FormStackProps {readonly children: ReactNode; readonly className?: string}`; `FormStack` renders `div` data-slot "form-stack" with `cn("flex flex-col gap-4 [&_[data-slot=form-item]]:gap-2", className)`.
- `interface FormActionsProps {readonly children: ReactNode; readonly align?: "start" | "end" | "between"; readonly variant?: "inline" | "dialog"; readonly className?: string}`; `FormActions` renders `div` data-slot "form-actions": inline (default) = "flex items-center gap-2 pt-2" plus justify-end for "end" / justify-between for "between"; dialog = "flex flex-col-reverse gap-2 pt-2 sm:flex-row sm:justify-end" (align ignored).
- Usage rule: the `<form>` element carries no spacing class; FormStack is its single layout child (or the single child of CardContent inside the form); FormActions is the last child of FormStack.
Setup steps (own their useForm): `AccountStep({onNext, loading})`, `BackupStep({onNext, onBack, onSkip, loading})`, `NotificationsStep({onNext, onBack, onSkip, loading})`, `ProxyStep({onSubmit, onBack, onSkip, loading, deployError})`, `SettingsStep({onNext, onBack, loading})`; today each renders `form > CardContent.space-y-4 (fields) + CardFooter (buttons; justify-between with Back on the left and Skip + submit on the right, AccountStep a single full-width Next)`.
</interfaces>
