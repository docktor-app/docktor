**GitHub issues:** [#91 Tab bar without a scrollbar](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/91) (UI-04, bundle [#96](https://github.com/docktor-app/docktor/issues/96)).

<objective>
Remove the scrollbar the user sees under the stack tab bar at desktop and tablet widths while keeping horizontal swipe scrolling, with the active tab scrolled into view, on narrow screens.

Per CONTEXT D-10 (fix the vertical overflow; hide the horizontal scrollbar; active tab scrolled into view; verify at three widths) and UI-SPEC R-05. RESEARCH Pattern 5 notes the trigger's absolutely positioned underline pseudo-element (not only the focus ring) is the likely source of the vertical overflow; the prescribed wrapper fix removes both, and plan 20-11's Playwright assertion (`scrollHeight <= clientHeight`) is the proof. `[id].tsx` is already ~90 lines, so the tab bar is extracted into a section component (UI-SPEC section 5 allows this, CLAUDE.md requires it).

Purpose: a clean tab bar on desktop and tablet that still works on a phone.
Output: `StackTabBar` with tests, a slimmer `[id].tsx`, and a jsdom guard test on the page.
</objective>
