<objective>
Organise the sidebar into labelled sections with a segment-aware active highlight, and make it usable as an icon rail and inside the mobile sheet. Per CONTEXT D-01 the approved `19-UI-SPEC.md` is the contract: a pure nav model plus `isNavItemActive` in `client/src/lib/sidebar-nav.ts` (UI-SPEC D-03), two sections Platform (Dashboard, Stacks, Templates) and System (Storage, Settings) per CONTEXT D-06 / UI-SPEC D-01, `collapsible="icon"` with tooltips and a logo-to-mark swap (UI-SPEC D-02), and the mobile sheet closing on internal navigation (UI-SPEC D-09). `components/ui/sidebar.tsx` is not edited; everything is props and class names on call sites.

Purpose: users find entries by section, keep their bearings on nested pages (stack detail, backups, create flow), and can collapse the sidebar or use it on a phone without losing navigation.
Output: `sidebar-nav.ts` and its unit test, the rewritten `AppSidebar` and its component test, a new desktop Playwright spec `sidebar.spec.ts`, and an extended mobile sidebar test.
</objective>
